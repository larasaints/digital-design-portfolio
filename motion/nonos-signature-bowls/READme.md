<h2>My Role</h2><p>I created the visual direction and developed the complete menu presentation, including:</p><ul><li><p>Layout and composition</p></li><li><p>Typography and text hierarchy</p></li><li><p>Product/menu information placement</p></li><li><p>Pricing and availability presentation</p></li><li><p>CTA placement</p></li><li><p>Consistent visual styling across all pages</p></li><li><p>Animated transitions between sections</p></li><li><p>Motion timing and pacing</p></li><li><p>Final video editing and sound-effect timing</p></li></ul>

<h2>Motion Design</h2>
<p>The static menu pages were developed into a motion graphic using animated transitions and carefully timed visual elements. The motion treatment was designed to: </p>
<ul><li><p>Guide viewers through each menu item</p></li>
<li><p>Create smooth transitions between pages</p></li>
<li><p>Designed to maintain attention while keeping information readable</p></li>
<li><p>Add movement to the promotional presentation</p></li>
<li><p>Lead viewers toward the final call-to-action</p></li></ul>
