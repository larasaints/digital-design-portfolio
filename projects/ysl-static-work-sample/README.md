## 🎨 My Role

I developed the entire visual identity composition, asset layout, and typographic alignment for this creative project, including:

* **Minimalist Spatial Framing:** Engineering an elegant, high-contrast composition that positions a signature red lipstick container over a soft, flowing white silk texture backdrop.
* **Typographic Architecture:** Blending a bold, classical serif script ("Dress") with a sharp, tracked italic serif subtitle ("your lips.") to establish immediate reading depth and luxury editorial appeal.
* **Ribbon Interlocking & Masking:** Splicing and weaving customized typographic brand ribbons ("YVES SAINT LAURENT ROUGE PUR") diagonally behind and across the central product to create a multi-dimensional layered layout.
* **Monochromatic Color Balancing:** Unifying the deep crimson tones of the cosmetics product with the typography and branding lines to generate a clean, bold visual statement against the neutral background fabric.

---

## 👁️ Visual Structure & Design Elements

The visual design is structured meticulously around minimalist editorial principles to communicate luxury and prestige:

* **The Focal Product Anchor:** Positioned slightly off-center along a dynamic diagonal tilt, utilizing the stark polished gold container to anchor light reflections and draw immediate visual attention.
* **Diagonal Vector Lines:** Leverages the sharp angles of the branding ribbons to guide the viewer’s eye path from the upper-left typography blocks down toward the bottom edge, framing the product cleanly.
* **Texture & Contrast Play:** Juxtaposes the solid metallic surfaces of the lipstick and the sharp lines of the ribbons against the organic, flowing folds of the fabric canvas.
