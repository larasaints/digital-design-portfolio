# 🎬 Bo's Coffee — Promotional Product Motion Graphic

A promotional product motion graphic created to showcase Bo's Coffee beverage offerings through targeted visual layouts, fast-paced transitions, striking typography, and strategic marketing messaging.
A high-impact promotional motion graphic project conceptualized to showcase drink products in a dynamic social media video format. This case study breaks down the creative strategy, editing process, and motion principles used to build engaging short-form marketing content.

## 🎨 My Role

I developed the entire visual direction and layout sequence for the product campaign, including:

* **Asset Enhancement & Framing:** Structuring clean graphic containers and high-contrast styling around lower-resolution, web-sourced beverage photography to preserve a premium visual standard.
* **Typography & Visual Scale:** Selecting bold headline typography to designate work sample milestones while mapping readable information fields on mobile panels.
* **Layout and Composition:** Aligning empty spatial zones with crisp brand assets to avoid over-cluttering mobile screen viewports.
* **Animated Transitions:** Sequencing rhythmic section movements and text revealing actions to keep the timeline dynamic.
* **Motion Pacing:** Synchronizing video cut structures tightly with background music tracks to anchor audience attention within the critical first three seconds.

---

## 🎬 Motion Design & Pacing Curves
Static beverage and branding components were built out into an active mobile motion graphic using multi-track timeline layers. The motion treatment focuses on balancing aggressive pacing with smooth visual execution:

* **Audio-Synchronized Pacing:** Implemented quick, snappy cuts timed tightly to the background music to capture attention within the critical first 3 seconds and retain fast-scrolling mobile viewers.
* **Fluid Animation Curves:** Applied custom ease-in and ease-out transitions to spatial movements, avoiding rigid, linear tracking to keep the visual flow professional and natural.
* **High-Contrast Readability:** Paired clean, bold typographic layers over deep product tones to ensure contextual information remains instantly glanceable on smaller smartphone displays.
* **Conversion Mapping:** Managed sequential movement vectors to guide the viewer’s perspective naturally toward the final closing call-to-action (CTA).
